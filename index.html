Questions

AI visibility, explained.

What is AI visibility?

How likely AI search engines — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity — are to find, trust, and recommend your business when people ask for recommendations. It depends on signals like whether AI crawlers can read your site, structured data that identifies your business, and content AI engines can quote.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO helps you rank in a list of links. AI visibility determines whether you're the answer itself. AI engines don't show ten blue links — they recommend a handful of businesses directly. It builds on SEO foundations but adds signals like AI-crawler access, entity data, llms.txt, and quotable content structure.

Does ChatGPT rank businesses?

Not with a public ranking — but when someone asks for the best provider near them, ChatGPT recommends specific businesses based on what it can read and verify across the web. Businesses with strong, machine-readable signals get named. The rest get left out of the answer.

Can local businesses benefit?

Local businesses have the most to gain. "Best plumber near me" is exactly what people ask AI assistants — and most local competitors haven't optimized yet, so early movers get recommended disproportionately often.

How often should I check my score?

After any meaningful site change, and monthly as a pulse-check. AI engines re-crawl regularly and your competitors' visibility changes too. It's free and takes under a minute.
