Questions

StackFlow, explained simply.

What is StackFlow?

StackFlow is an AI growth platform for small businesses. It helps owners confidently adopt AI with a personalized strategy (the AI Growth Blueprint), an AI Visibility Score, curated tool recommendations by business need, and a library of ready-to-use automations.

How does StackFlow help my business adopt AI?

You follow a guided path: assess where you stand, get a prioritized plan, choose the right tools for your goals and budget, then automate repetitive work — no technical background required.

Is StackFlow free to start?

Yes — you can start for free with no credit card. StackFlow also recommends paid third-party tools and may earn an affiliate commission when you buy through some links, at no extra cost to you.

What is the AI Visibility Score?

It measures how prepared your business is to be found and recommended by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity — and gives you a prioritized list of what to fix.

Which AI tools does StackFlow recommend?

Curated tools organized by business need — writing, SEO, automation, video, meetings, customer support, analytics, CRM, design, and email — chosen for fit, not commission rates.
