Who we are

StackFlow ("we," "us") operates trystackflow.com, an AI growth platform for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email hello@trystackflow.com.

What we collect

We collect information you choose to give us:

  • Contact and form data. When you request an AI Visibility Audit, check your AI Visibility Score, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us, we collect what you submit — typically your name, email address, website, and details about your business.
  • Usage data. If we use website analytics, we collect standard, aggregated usage information (pages visited, referral source, device type). We do not sell this data.

We do not collect payment information on this site. Purchases of third-party tools happen on those vendors' own websites under their own policies.

How we use it

  • We only add you to our tips newsletter if you tick the separate opt-in box when requesting a report (or subscribe elsewhere on the site). Every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe.

  • To understand what content is useful so we can improve the site.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not share your personal information with the third-party tool vendors we recommend. If you click an affiliate link, that vendor's own privacy policy applies on their site.

Service providers

We may use trusted service providers (such as an email marketing platform or form processor) to operate the site. They process your data only on our instructions and for the purposes above.

Cookies & third-party requests

This site keeps cookies to a minimum. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and if analytics is enabled it may set or read identifiers as described by that provider. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Some links on this site are affiliate links — we may earn a commission when you purchase through them, at no additional cost to you. See our full Affiliate Disclosure.

Your choices & rights

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in any email. You can also email us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and we'll act on the request promptly.

Data retention & security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take care with what you share.

Changes
