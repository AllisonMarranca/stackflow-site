Test your visibility

Paste these into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity. Run each in all four — the differences are revealing.

The recommendation testThe single most important prompt — is it you, or your competitor?
I'm looking for the best [SERVICE, e.g. residential roofing company] in [CITY, STATE]. Who would you recommend and why? Please name specific businesses.
The brand knowledge testDoes AI actually know who you are?
What can you tell me about [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] in [CITY]? What do they do, who are they for, and what is their reputation?
The comparison testSee how AI frames you against a named competitor.
Compare [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] and [COMPETITOR NAME] for someone in [CITY] who needs [SERVICE]. Which would you lean toward, and why?
The objection testDiscover what AI thinks your weaknesses are.
I'm considering hiring [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] for [SERVICE]. What should I be cautious about or verify before choosing them?
The source testFind out where AI gets its information about you — then fix those sources.
What sources of information exist online about [YOUR BUSINESS NAME] in [CITY]? Where would you look to verify their services, pricing, and reputation?

Fix your content

Use these to produce content AI engines can quote — the fastest wins after a low Visibility Score.

FAQ generatorBuilds the Q&A content AI engines lift verbatim.
I run [BUSINESS TYPE] in [CITY]. Generate the 8 questions potential customers most likely ask an AI assistant before hiring a business like mine. For each, write a 2-4 sentence answer in my voice that leads with a direct, specific answer (real numbers and timeframes where sensible), then adds context. Here are facts about my business to use: [PASTE KEY FACTS: services, pricing ranges, service area, years in business, differentiators].
Quotable service page rewriteTurns vague marketing copy into answers AI can cite.
Rewrite this service page so an AI engine could quote it as a direct answer about what we do, for whom, where, and at what price range. Keep my brand voice, remove vague claims, add specifics. State the service, area, and process plainly in the first two sentences. Page copy: [PASTE YOUR CURRENT PAGE TEXT]
llms.txt generatorDrafts the file that tells AI engines what to cite you for.
Create an llms.txt file for my business following the llms.txt convention: an H1 with the business name, a one-paragraph blockquote summary, a "Key pages" section with markdown links and one-line descriptions, and a short "What we should be cited for" list. My business: [NAME]. Website: [DOMAIN]. What we do: [SERVICES]. Area: [CITY/REGION]. Key pages: [LIST YOUR MAIN PAGE URLS].
Google Business Profile descriptionYour GBP is a top source AI engines read about local businesses.
Write a 750-character Google Business Profile description for [BUSINESS NAME], a [BUSINESS TYPE] in [CITY]. Lead with what we do and where, include our main services as plain phrases people search for, mention [YEARS] years in business and [KEY DIFFERENTIATOR]. No hype words, no exclamation points.

Local business

"Near me" questions are the ones AI answers with specific business names — local has the most to win.

The "near me" sweepRun your top 5 customer questions in one shot.
Answer each of these as if a real person in [CITY, STATE] asked you, naming specific local businesses in each answer:
1. Who's the best [SERVICE] near me?
2. Most affordable [SERVICE] in [CITY]?
3. [SERVICE] with the best reviews in [CITY]?
4. Fastest/emergency [SERVICE] in [CITY]?
5. Who should I trust for [HIGHER-END VARIANT OF SERVICE] in [CITY]?
Citation consistency auditInconsistent name/address/phone across the web erodes AI's confidence in you.
My business is listed across Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, and industry directories. Here is the name, address, and phone from each listing: [PASTE EACH LISTING'S NAP]. Identify every inconsistency, rank them by how much they could confuse a machine trying to verify my business, and give me the exact canonical version to standardize on.
Service-area page outlineOne strong page per city beats one weak page listing twenty.
Outline a service-area page for [BUSINESS NAME] serving [NEARBY CITY]. It must be genuinely useful, not doorway-page filler: local proof (projects, customers, landmarks we serve near), specifics of providing [SERVICE] in that area, an FAQ with 3 location-specific questions, and a clear call to action. Flag anything that would look like thin duplicate content.

Schema & technical

The machine-readable layer. (Prefer ready-made templates? They're all in the Fix-It Kit, included with the AI Visibility Audit.)

Schema generatorProduces the LocalBusiness JSON-LD your Visibility Score checks for.
Generate valid LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema for my website's homepage. Use the most specific @type that fits (e.g. Plumber, Dentist, LegalService). Include name, url, description, telephone, address, areaServed, openingHours, and sameAs links. My details: [NAME / DOMAIN / DESCRIPTION / PHONE / ADDRESS / AREAS / HOURS / SOCIAL & GBP LINKS]. Then tell me exactly where to paste it and how to validate it.
robots.txt reviewOne Disallow line can make you invisible to an entire AI engine.
Review this robots.txt and tell me: (1) which AI crawlers — GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, CCBot — are blocked or allowed, (2) whether anything blocks normal search engines, and (3) the corrected file if changes are needed. My robots.txt: [PASTE CONTENTS OF yourdomain.com/robots.txt]
Competitor gap analysisReverse-engineer why AI prefers them.
Here is the text of my homepage: [PASTE]. Here is the text of my competitor's homepage: [PASTE]. From an AI engine's perspective, list every signal their page gives that mine doesn't — specificity, proof, structure, answerable questions — ranked by impact. Then give me the five highest-impact changes to my page.
