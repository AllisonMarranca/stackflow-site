What is "AI visibility," exactly?

It's how likely AI assistants are to find, trust, and recommend your business when someone asks them a relevant question. It draws on the same foundations as SEO — accurate information, strong reputation signals, and well-structured content — applied to how generative engines answer.

Which platforms do you check?

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity — the assistants your customers are most likely to ask for recommendations.

Do I need a big marketing budget to improve?

No. Many of the highest-impact fixes are about accuracy and structure, not ad spend. Your report prioritizes changes by impact so you can start with quick wins.
