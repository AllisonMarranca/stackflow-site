Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

What is AI visibility?

How likely AI engines — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity — are to find, trust, and recommend your business when people ask them for recommendations. It's the new front door: AI names a handful of businesses instead of showing ten links.

How is the score calculated?

We run a live scan of 12 signals with published weights: AI crawler access in your robots.txt (25 pts), business schema (15), llms.txt (10), HTTPS reachability (10), plus FAQ schema, entity links, title, description, headings, social preview, robots.txt and sitemap. No black box — every point is itemized in your results.

How long does it take?

About 10 seconds for the scan; under a minute total. The full Audit is hand-reviewed by a specialist and arrives within 1 business day.

Is this really free?

Yes — the score, the findings, and the fixes are free, and you don't need to give us an email to see them. The emailed copy of your report and the deeper Audit are optional.

Why upgrade to the Audit?

The free score tells you what's broken on your site. The Audit tells you what's happening in your market: who AI actually recommends when asked, how you compare to competitors, where your traffic and keywords stand, and a prioritized 30-day plan — reviewed by a Mainstreethost specialist, with a strategy session included. The Audit is $29; the Pro Audit ($149) adds agency-grade analysis — citations, traffic, keyword gaps, local and technical SEO — plus a 45-minute strategy session. Both carry a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Which platforms are analyzed?

The signals we scan are the ones used by ChatGPT (GPTBot), Claude (ClaudeBot), Perplexity (PerplexityBot), Google AI Overviews and Gemini (Google-Extended), plus the Common Crawl dataset (CCBot) that many models train on.
